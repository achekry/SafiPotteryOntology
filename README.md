## Overview

Ontologies provide a standardized, machine-readable way to structure and formalize domain knowledge, enabling documentation, preservation, and sharing of cultural heritage. Safi pottery, a Moroccan craft with significant cultural and social value, lacks a formal ontology, limiting its digital representation and accessibility for research, education, and heritage documentation.

This SafiPotteryOntology presents a domain ontology for Safi pottery, modeling artisans, techniques, raw materials, tools, product types, workshops, and cooperatives. The ontology was manually constructed in Protégé using OWL and RDF, following ontology engineering best practices and combining fieldwork, artisan interviews, and archival resources. Competency questions (CQs) guided the design, and SPARQL queries were executed to demonstrate practical use cases. The ontology was also informally reviewed by local experts to ensure terminological and cultural accuracy.

The resulting model provides a structured, extensible framework for preserving and organizing Safi pottery knowledge. It supports educational applications, research, and digital heritage initiatives, while offering a methodological reference for future semantic modeling of traditional crafts.
